Ashborne Partners is founded on over a decade of experience placing leaders across SaaS, AI, and Fortune 500 companies. That background shapes a simple belief: most executive search confuses access with insight. Having a large database of names is not the same as understanding which one belongs in a specific seat, at a specific moment, inside a specific culture.

We are a deliberately focused firm. We specialise in sales and revenue leadership — Chief Revenue Officers, VPs of Sales, and the senior commercial talent that determines whether a company hits its number. That focus is on the discipline, not a single sector: the same revenue leadership drives growth across SaaS, AI, financial services, and the other industries building and selling technology. We go deep on the function rather than spreading thin across unrelated specialisms.

When we take on an assignment, we take it completely.

Mapping

The whole market, not the reachable part

We map every plausible operator in a market before we speak to anyone — not just those who are visible, active, or already known to us. Coverage is a machine problem. We treat it like one.

Signal

Reading a career, not scanning a CV

We track the things that actually predict a revenue leader: the shape of the number they carried, the motion they inherited versus the one they built, and what happened after they left.

Judgement

The shortlist is never automated

Every candidate we present has been spoken to, pressure-tested, and argued over by a partner. No model has ever made that call here, and none will.

The question is not whether a search firm uses AI — by now they all do. The question is where they stop letting it decide. We stop at the shortlist.

How We Work

"Most searches fail not in the finding — but in the understanding of what was truly needed."

I
Immersion

Before we approach a single candidate, we spend time understanding your organisation at depth — strategy, culture, the unspoken politics, and the precise conditions under which the next leader will succeed or fail.

II
Pursuit

We target individuals who are not searching. The best leaders rarely are. Our network and reputation give us access to people who will take our call because of who we are, not what we are offering.

III
Counsel

We advise both sides through the process — honestly. We tell clients when a candidate is not right. We tell candidates when a role is not right for them. This is how we maintain trust across decades.
